Quick answers

Where is the preview? Open the compose box on x.com (click Post, or press N). A "Preview" button appears in the toolbar next to the Post button — click it and the panel opens. It never opens on its own.
The Preview button isn't showing. Make sure the extension is enabled (click its toolbar icon — the switch at the top should be on), then reload the x.com tab. The button appears only in compose dialogs, not on the inline home-timeline composer.
Long tweets get cut off with "Show more". That's intentional — it's exactly what the real timeline does past 280 characters. Click "Show more" in the preview to expand, "Show less" to fold it back.
Can I change the tweets shown around my draft? Yes — click the extension icon and paste up to four tweet links under "Timeline tweets". They're fetched on your device and rendered in place.
Does it post anything or read my DMs? No. It only reads the compose box on the page to render the preview, entirely locally. See the privacy policy — nothing leaves your browser.

When reporting a bug

Include your Chrome version, what you were composing (text-only, media, thread, reply), and a screenshot if possible. That's usually enough to fix it fast.
