Add to Chrome

What it does

  • Live preview inside x.com. A Preview button appears next to Post; click it and your draft renders in a real timeline — updating with every keystroke.
  • Phone and desktop frames. True iPhone proportions or the X web timeline in a browser window, so you see exact line breaks and image crops.
  • Accurate character counting. URLs count 23, emoji and CJK count 2 — and long posts clamp behind a working "Show more", up to 25,000 characters, just like X.
  • Media, threads, replies. Images, videos and GIFs appear instantly; threads stack with the connector line; replies show "Replying to".
  • Your timeline context. Paste any tweet links and they're fetched on your device and rendered around your draft.

Privacy

No account, no analytics, no third-party servers — everything runs locally in your browser. Read the privacy policy.
